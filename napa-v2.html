The Process

From Photo to
Nutrition, in Seconds

NAPA V2 turns a single image into a complete, structured nutrition profile — built for the way dal is actually cooked and eaten.

1
Capture

Snap or upload a photo of your dal in a bowl, katori, or plate — however you'd normally eat it.

2
Detect

NAPA identifies the dal variety, or mix of varieties, and estimates the portion in the frame.

3
Analyze

Our nutrition model maps the visual read to a verified nutrient database, accounting for oil and tempering.

4
Insights

Get the full breakdown plus personalized suggestions, synced straight into the Dietary Guide app.

What It Can Do

Built for How
India Actually Eats Dal

Not a generic food scanner — a model shaped around dal, the way it's cooked, mixed, and served across Indian kitchens.

Multi-Dal Detection

Recognizes multiple dals and mixed preparations — like mixed-dal khichdi — in a single frame.

Portion Estimation

Approximates serving size directly from the image — no manual weighing or entry required.

Macro & Micro Breakdown

Protein, carbs, fiber, iron, folate and more — estimated per serving, not just per 100g.

Recipe-Aware Context

Accounts for tadka, ghee, and oil in mixed dishes instead of scoring dal in isolation.

Instant Results

Analysis returns in seconds, right on your phone — fast enough to use at the table.

Privacy by Design

Images are processed to extract nutrition data only — never stored for training without consent.

Supported Varieties

Toor Dal Moong Dal Masoor Dal Chana Dal Urad Dal Rajma Chickpeas Green Gram Horse Gram Kidney Beans + more every release
